Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Sync my storage data accesses, uses, stores, shares, and deletes information obtained through Google OAuth and Google APIs.

1. Information the application may access

The application requests only permissions presented to the user on Google's OAuth consent screen. Depending on the permissions granted, the application may access:

  • Basic Google account information, such as account identifier, name, email address, and profile information, when included in the authorized scopes.
  • Google Drive metadata, such as file or folder names, identifiers, types, modification times, and hierarchy information required to identify data for synchronization.
  • Google Drive file content when access to file content is required to perform a synchronization requested or configured by the user.
  • Google Drive write permissions, if authorized, solely to create, update, or synchronize data as requested or configured by the user.
  • OAuth authorization data, including access or refresh tokens, when necessary to maintain the user's authorized connection.

2. How Google user data is used

Google user data is used only to provide the application's user-facing functionality, including:

  • authenticating and identifying the user;
  • establishing and maintaining the Google connection authorized by the user;
  • listing, locating, reading, and synchronizing authorized storage data;
  • performing write operations only when requested or configured by the user and allowed by the granted permissions;
  • maintaining the security and reliability of the service.

Google user data is not used for advertising, profiling for advertising, or any purpose unrelated to the functionality described above.

3. Storage and retention

  • OAuth tokens may be stored securely for as long as needed to maintain the Google connection authorized by the user.
  • Google user data may be processed or temporarily cached only as necessary to perform synchronization and operate the service.
  • Data synchronized at the user's direction may remain in the storage destination selected or configured by the user and is subject to that destination's retention settings.
  • The application does not retain Google user data longer than reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, except where retention is required by law.

4. Sharing and transfer of data

Sync my storage data does not sell Google user data and does not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

Google user data may be transferred only in the following limited circumstances:

  • At the user's direction: to a destination or service selected or configured by the user as part of the synchronization functionality.
  • Service operation: to infrastructure or service providers only when necessary to operate, secure, or maintain the application, and only subject to appropriate confidentiality and security obligations.
  • Legal requirements: when disclosure is required by applicable law or a valid legal process.

5. Google API Services User Data Policy

Sync my storage data's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy , including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are used to protect OAuth credentials and Google user data against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. Users' Google passwords are never collected by this application.

7. Revoking access and deleting data

Users may stop the application's access to Google data at any time by:

  • revoking the application's permissions in their Google Account security or third-party access settings; and/or
  • requesting deletion of data controlled by the application through the developer contact below.

After access is revoked or a valid deletion request is received, stored OAuth tokens and application-controlled copies of Google user data that are no longer required for the service will be deleted, except where retention is required by law.

8. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application's functionality or data practices change. The effective date shown above will be updated when material changes are made.
